Occupancy conflicts internally: page4 states two people, pages6/8 state one. Retain this uncertainty. Rent is not specified: the handbook says fees change by application round. This is owner-provided information, not a current official dormitory notice, and remains marked for confirmation with the dormitory.

The handbook's map places this facility near Lotus/Bang Krathuek, outside the main university campus.
